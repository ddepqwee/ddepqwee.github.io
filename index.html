NEON BOUNCE

A new satisfying physics simulation every day.

What is this?

Neon Bounce is a YouTube Shorts channel with procedurally generated physics simulations: a ball escaping spinning neon rings, where every bounce plays the next note of a classical melody. The visuals, physics and music are all generated by code written for this channel.

Neon Bounce Uploader

The channel owner uses a small private desktop tool, Neon Bounce Uploader, to publish the generated videos to their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data. Read the Privacy Policy.
